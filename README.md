# 🍹 Bar Simulator

**Atlantis Bar** is a fast-paced, arcade-style 3D bar management and cocktail-making game inspired by the chaotic time-management gameplay of *Overcooked*.

Step behind the counter, manage a busy bar, prepare cocktails, and serve demanding customers before their patience runs out. Handle multiple drink orders, move between preparation stations, manage inventory, and keep the party going during increasingly hectic shifts.

### 🎮 Core Features

- **Cocktail Making:** Prepare drinks through four interactive activities: Glass & Ice, Pour & Measure, Mix, and Garnish & Serve.
- **Fast-Paced Gameplay:** Handle multiple customer orders simultaneously under time pressure.
- **Bar Management:** Manage ingredients, bottles, glassware, inventory, and bar operations.
- **Customer AI:** Customers have individual preferences, patience, spending limits, and behaviours.
- **Staff Management:** Hire bartenders, barbacks, and bouncers to improve bar operations.
- **Security System:** Handle occasional customer arguments and fights.
- **Upgrades & Progression:** Upgrade equipment, hire staff, unlock recipes, and expand your bar.
- **Combo & Scoring System:** Earn tips, build service combos, complete shift objectives, and achieve star ratings.
- **Campaign Mode:** Progress through five unique bar venues, each introducing new challenges.

### 🍸 Gameplay Experience

Every shift is a new challenge. Prioritize orders, prepare drinks accurately, manage your resources, and keep customers satisfied. As the bar gets busier, efficient movement, quick decisions, and precise cocktail preparation become essential.

### 🛠️ Technology Stack

- Three.js
- TypeScript
- Vite
- JavaScript / Web Technologies

### 🎯 Game Vision

Create an engaging, colorful, and chaotic bar-management experience that combines hands-on cocktail preparation with strategic time management, customer interactions, and business progression.

**Run the bar. Master the cocktails. Survive the rush!**
